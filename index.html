Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a powerful PC to run this?

No. StreamCut Pro is optimized to run on standard modern PCs or laptops with Windows 10/11. It utilizes lightweight multi-threaded rendering and stream chunking without straining your machine.

Is this compliant with YouTube's Terms of Service?

Yes. StreamCut Pro connects to YouTube through the official Google YouTube Data API v3 and Google Cloud OAuth2 credentials. You own and control your own upload credentials.

Can I change which streamers the software monitors?

Yes! In the streamers.json config file, you can add any Twitch streamer usernames (GTA RP, Call of Duty, Minecraft, Valorant, or Just Chatting). The bot will automatically monitor them.
